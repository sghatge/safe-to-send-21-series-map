Safe to Send · Ep 21 — Three weeks, one map

Week 3 retro. Twenty-one small open-source repos so far, which is great for learning and terrible for anyone trying to follow along.

So this repo is a map. One registry file lists every episode and which earlier episode it builds on. A script validates it and generates an index with a dependency diagram GitHub draws by itself.

Looking at the map, the series keeps coming back to three ideas:
1. Gate the action, not the prompt.
2. Rules decide, models explain, tests prove it.
3. Standardise before you automate.

Fifteen of the 28 episodes build directly on an earlier one. Nothing here was planned as a framework. It became one by shipping a small piece every day.

The final week asks: is your team actually ready, and how do you measure what any of this saves?
